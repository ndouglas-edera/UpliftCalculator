Biggest opportunities in your result

Once you calculate your opportunity, this section will show which components contribute most to the estimate.

1. Engineering capacity

We estimate how much engineering time is currently spent operating AI execution infrastructure and repeating environment setup.

Infrastructure operations hours
SRE engineers × maintenance percentage × 2,080 working hours
The model assumes 2,080 working hours per engineer per year and applies the percentage of time you said is spent maintaining AI execution infrastructure.
Recoverable operations hours
Operations hours × (22% + data signal + control signal)
The model assumes that a baseline portion of infrastructure operations can be simplified, with additional opportunity where workloads involve more sensitive data or require stronger infrastructure control.
Repeated setup hours
Monthly workloads × 12 × initialisation time × repeat rate
This estimates the annual engineering time associated with repeatedly initialising environments that could potentially be reused or restored from state.
Engineering value
Recoverable engineering hours × $165,000 / 2,080
Engineering hours are converted into economic value using an illustrative annual fully-loaded engineering cost of $165,000.

2. Infrastructure efficiency

We estimate an efficiency opportunity against your stated annual infrastructure spend.

Infrastructure opportunity
Spend × min(28%, 8% + repeat rate × 50% + fork rate × 25%)
The model caps this opportunity at 28% of infrastructure spend. Higher repetition and more trajectory branching increase the estimated opportunity because reusable state can potentially reduce duplicated work.

3. Execution efficiency

This estimates the economic value associated with reducing repeated environment initialisation.

Execution opportunity
min(22% of spend, workloads × init time × repeat rate × 12% × $35)
The result is capped at 22% of annual infrastructure spend. The workload-based calculation increases as workload volume, initialisation time and repeated setup increase.

4. Data & control opportunity

Security-sensitive and controlled execution requirements add another directional signal to the opportunity estimate.

Security signal
Non-sensitive: 1% · Internal: 3% · Customer/regulated: 6%
Higher data sensitivity produces a larger signal because controlled execution environments can become more valuable when workloads need access to sensitive data.
Control opportunity
Infrastructure spend × security signal [+ 4% if infrastructure control is required]
If you require workloads to remain within infrastructure or network boundaries you control, the model adds an additional 4% signal to the opportunity.

5. Total opportunity

The headline figure combines the four opportunity components.

Estimated annual business opportunity
Engineering value + Infrastructure efficiency + Execution efficiency + Control opportunity
The result is best effort for uplift gains. It is not a real prediction of savings, Edera pricing, or guaranteed customer outcomes. Request a personalised assessment for a quotation.

6. Illustrative ROI

Potential value / estimated adoption cost
Total opportunity ÷ (25% × annual infrastructure spend)
For this assessment, the calculator assumes an illustrative adoption cost equal to 25% of current annual infrastructure spend. This is a modelling assumption, not Edera-specific pricing.
Important: The values and percentages are a best-effort designed to better understand uplift opportunities. Request a personal assessment that is already calibrated using validated Edera benchmarks and industry data for an agreed value methodology.
